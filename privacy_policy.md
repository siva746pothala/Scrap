# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

At **Scrap Memories**, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Our App operates as an invite-only secure vault system, designed to encrypt and synchronize private memories (such as photos, drawings, and voice clips) within closed groups of trusted members ("Squads").

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Receive

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to run the App's vault synchronization services.

### A. Account Information
* **Email Address:** Used solely for authentication, password recovery, and secure registration.
* **Username & Name:** Displayed to other members of your private Squad rooms to identify your contributions.
* **Profile Photo:** An optional display avatar shown inside your shared Squad rooms.

### B. User-Generated Content (Canvas Content)
* **Photos & Drawings:** Shared images uploaded by you onto the shared canvas workspace.
* **Voice Recordings:** Audio clips recorded inside the App to place sound notes on the workspace.
* **Doodles & Texts:** Digital drawings or text stickers added to the canvas boards.

---

## 2. Permissions We Request

To provide core interactive features, the App requires permission to access the following hardware components on your mobile device:

* **Camera Permission:** Required to capture live photos using your device camera, select profile pictures, and scan secure room invitation QR codes.
* **Microphone Permission:** Required to record and post voice notes onto canvas boards.
* **Storage Access:** Required to save exported vault backups or select existing pictures from your camera roll.

*Note: We only access these services when you actively interact with the corresponding features inside the App.*

---

## 3. Data Encryption and Technical Security

We prioritize the security of your private memories:
* **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE):** Canvas content and room handshakes are secured using industry-standard symmetric block ciphers (AES-GCM 256-bit) and private RSA key pairs managed locally on your device.

---

## 4. User-Generated Content (UGC) Safety Policy

We promote a safe and respectful environment:
* **Muting and Blocking:** You can block/mute other users inside your rooms at any time.
* **Instant Moderation:** Any member can flag (🚩) an entry, which instantly deletes it globally from the shared canvas board.
* **Content Rules:** Uploading illegal, abusive, harmful, or sexually explicit content is strictly prohibited and will lead to account termination.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

* **No Third-Party Trackers:** We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties.
* **First-Party Advertisements:** The App may display internal promotional banners configured directly on our secure servers. No user behavior or private data is shared with external ad agencies.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your content remains active as long as you participate in your Squad rooms. 

* **Instant Content Deletion:** If you delete a photo, voice note, or entry from the canvas, it is immediately and permanently removed from our active sync servers.
* **Account Deletion (In-App):** You can permanently delete your account and clear all associated data directly through the App settings (`Vault Settings > Delete My Account`) at any time. 
* **Account Deletion (Web Request):** If you are unable to access the App, you can request account and data deletion by contacting us at our developer support email address.
* **Data Removed Upon Deletion:** When you delete your account, your personal information (email, username, profile name, profile image) and all private shared canvas content uploaded by your account are permanently and immediately erased from our databases. We do not retain backup archives of deleted accounts.

---

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us. You may reach us at your registered Play Console developer support email address.
